Marina Manager — Feature List

Campbell's Landing Marina · everything the app does · spec v2026-10-02
Anything wrong, missing, or not needed? Hit note on that row.

Day to day, on the boat

  • F-01Cards queueAll open work, filtered by status, boat or customer
  • F-02Card detailThe whole card on one screen — no jumping between tabs
  • F-03New cardScan the boat's QR, or fill the paper form in by hand
  • F-04Received itemsTick off what the customer handed over — battery, keys, cover, paddles, life jackets, cushions, gas cans, tie ropes, lights
  • F-05Authorised workOil change, outdrive service, tune up, lower unit drain, prop rebuild — approved, done, by whom, when, what products
  • F-06Condition assessmentRate six areas — top, hull, upholstery, motor, propeller, lower unit
  • F-07ChecklistsFall, Spring and Storage checklists, recorded per employee
  • F-08Work logsDated notes, with handwriting in a photo read automatically
  • F-09Parts usedPart number, description, quantity
  • F-10PhotosTyped, captioned, and stamped with where it was taken. Needs signal.
  • F-11MapWhere every boat physically is — building, boathouse, slip, row, column

Office

  • F-12Customers & boatsOne screen — contact details, boats, engine and hull serials, licence numbers, length
  • F-13No duplicatesMatching email or phone offers the existing customer instead of creating a second one
  • F-14InvoicesLine items, quantities, tax rate, totals
  • F-15Issue an invoiceNumbers it, marks it issued, locks it to the card
  • F-16Customer QR linkCustomer scans their copy of the card to see progress. Can be revoked instantly.
  • F-17Product catalogueProducts and service templates, editable without an app release

Admin

  • F-18EmployeesAdd people, set their job title, deactivate them
  • F-19Storage layoutBuildings, boathouses, slips, rows, columns — change the marina's layout and the app follows
  • F-20BackupAdmin-only export and restore

In the field

  • F-21Works with no signalEverything opens. Everything you type is saved on the phone.
  • F-22PIN unlock offlineYour PIN opens the app in the yard. Expires after a set window.
  • F-23Pending countSetup shows how many changes are still waiting to upload
  • F-24Two people, one cardBadge says "Needs review". You pick which version wins — in the yard, no signal needed.

How it looks and runs

  • F-25Phone & desktopPhone layout on a phone; proper two-pane list-and-detail on a desktop. Not a stretched phone.
  • F-26Light & darkBoth designed properly, not one flipped into the other
  • F-27Readable in sunlightBig targets, high contrast, works with gloves, scales to 200% text
  • F-28InstallableLives on the home screen. No app store, no install prompt.

Deliberately not included

  • F-29Not in v1Payments · scheduling and bay bookings · parts inventory and ordering · customer login or messaging · reports and dashboards · a second marina · bulk actions across many cards
