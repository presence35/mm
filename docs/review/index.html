Layer 1 — Top level

What the app is, what you get on the home bar, and what it deliberately will never do.

Navigation L1-NAV

Five destinations. M3 allows five, so nothing else gets promoted into the bar.

Today: Cards, Customers, Boats, Map, SettingsCards, People, Map, Setup, Scan
−Customers and Boats are two separate screensPeople — customers and their boats, one screen
−Settings (theme, account)Setup — theme, offline state, sync queue, account
+Scan — its own destination (camera → new card, or customer QR link)
You feel it: fewer taps to a customer. Setup now shows how many changes are still waiting to upload — visible trust, not a hidden spinner.
Rec Approve.

Five job titles collapse to three L1-ROLES

The biggest change in the app. Cleaning and wrapping stop being jobs someone holds.

−admin · office · mechanic · cleaner · wrapper
·adminadmin — everything: employees, catalogue, templates, backup
·officeoffice — customers, boats, cards, invoices. No employees, no catalogue.
·mechanicmechanic — work on cards: tasks, checklists, condition, logs, photos. No customers, no invoicing, no Setup.
You feel it: nobody logs in as "cleaner" any more. Cleaning and wrapping are tasks on a card, recorded with the name of whoever did them. Fewer logins, fewer forgotten passwords.
Rec Approve. Attribution is already recorded per task, so accountability survives — see OQ-1 for the gap it leaves.

The app works with no signal L1-OFFLINE

Offline is not an error state. It is the normal assumption.

~Open the app without signal → little or nothing loadsEverything already on the phone opens instantly. Work is saved on the phone and uploads when signal returns.
You feel it: the yard, the boat, the engine bay — no dead spots. Nothing you typed is ever lost by losing signal mid-sentence.
Rec Approve. This is the reason the app is being rebuilt.

What this app will never do L1-SCOPE

Locked out deliberately, so nobody expects it later.

−Payments or card processing · scheduling / bay reservations · parts inventory & purchasing · customer accounts or messaging · reports & dashboards · a second marina · bulk operations across many cards
You feel it: invoices are raised and issued, but money does not move through the app. No bulk-select fifty cards and tick them all off.
Rec Approve with one exception — see OQ-2. Bulk invoicing is the most likely thing to hurt in winter.

Layer 2 — Rules that change the daily work

Six behaviours where the app will refuse, block, or ask a human to decide.

Photos need signal L2-PHOTOS

~Camera always available; photos wait silentlyCamera always available to look with. Taking a photo with no signal gives a clear "Waiting for signal" block with a retry — it never looks like a saved photo.
You feel it: you can always inspect a boat, but a damage photo taken in a dead spot has to be re-shot. Silently queuing photos is unsafe — phones discard app data after ~7 days unused and the photo vanishes.
Rec Approve the block. Ask for OQ-3: let the worker at least note "damage photo pending" as text so the visit is not lost.

Office and admin actions refuse offline L2-OFFICE

+Anything gated to office or admin is blocked with no signal, and says why. Mechanic work queues normally.
You feel it: an admin cannot raise an invoice from a dead spot, and cannot add an employee. They have to find signal first.
Rec Approve. If someone was demoted while a phone sat in a locker, queuing their invoicing under old permissions is the worst case in the app.

Two people edit one card L2-CONFLICT

+Card gets a "Needs review" badge. Both versions are kept. You pick keep mine / keep theirs / edit and merge — and you can do it with no signal.
You feel it: the app never silently picks a winner over your notes. You see the clash and settle it.
Rec Approve. Settling a conflict in the yard without hunting for signal is the point.

Records older than 30 days leave the phone L2-RETENTION

+A phone keeps anything it has worked on, plus anything changed in the last 30 days. Older untouched records are dropped from the phone only — never from the server.
You feel it: a card a given phone never touched may not be on that phone after 30 days. It is always still on the server and comes back with one tap on signal.
Rec Change — see OQ-4. Cards currently in storage should never be evicted, however quiet.

The customer QR view shows less than today L2-QR

Scan the QR on the customer's copy of the card. No password, by design — so it is treated as a security surface, not a convenience.

·Customer name, boat, work order, status, servicesCustomer name, boat, work order, status, services, history
−Any internal notes, employee names, condition ratings, invoice lines or totals, work log text, photos
+The link can be revoked instantly. A wrong, revoked or guessed link looks exactly like a real one.
You feel it: the customer sees progress, not prices, photos, or staff opinion. Any QR link the card has issued can be killed in one tap.
Rec Approve, pending OQ-5 — office to confirm customers accept no pricing on the link.

Opening the app offline uses a PIN, not a fingerprint L2-UNLOCK

~Sign in with PIN while onlineOnce you've signed in with signal at least once, the same PIN opens the app offline. It expires after a set window and asks again.
You feel it: one PIN, works everywhere, never surprises you mid-shift. No "biometrics unavailable" dead end on a work phone.
Rec Approve. Fingerprint unlock silently fails to "must be online" on plenty of devices — exactly the failure this app cannot afford.

Test cards leak into the customer view L2-FAKE

+Test/demo cards still hand out real customer QR links, marked "FAKE DATA".
~Recommendation: never issue a customer link on a test card at all.
You feel it: near zero. But a real customer could be shown a demo boat's status.
Rec Change — refuse the link. One-line fix.

Open questions — need an answer before build finishes

Seven things only the office can decide. Type straight into the box.

OQ-1 · Nobody owns a card

Work is a queue filtered by status — it is not assigned to a person. Is that right, or should a card have a named owner?

OQ-2 · No bulk actions

Issuing winter invoices one card at a time. Keep it that way, or bring bulk back for v1?

OQ-3 · Blocked photo

When a photo can't upload, may the worker leave a text note instead ("damage photo pending") so the visit isn't lost?

OQ-4 · Cards in storage

Confirm: a card currently sitting in storage is never dropped from a phone, however long it's been quiet.

OQ-5 · Customer link has no prices

Does the customer need to see the invoice on the QR link, or is the paper/email invoice enough?

OQ-6 · Backward moves on the lifecycle

List the "go back" moves you really use — e.g. ready → service because a fault was found.

OQ-7 · Where do the master lists get edited?

Name the screen. Candidates: a new Admin area, or inside Setup.
